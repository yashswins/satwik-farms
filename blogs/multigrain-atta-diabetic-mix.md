# Millet Master: Our Diabetic-Friendly Multigrain Atta

Our Millet Master Diabetic Mix is a 100% wheat-free flour blend specifically engineered for glycemic control. By replacing simple carbohydrates with complex starches and high-quality plant proteins, this flour transforms a staple meal into a therapeutic tool.

## How This Blend Works for Diabetics

### Glucose Spike Prevention

The combination of **Jowar** and **Bajra** provides complex carbohydrates that break down slowly. Unlike wheat — which can cause rapid insulin spikes — these millets ensure a steady, slow release of energy into the bloodstream.

### Enhanced Insulin Sensitivity

With **Soy Flour** and **Whole Chickpea Flour**, this mix is exceptionally high in protein. Protein-rich meals stimulate the secretion of incretin hormones, which help the body use its own insulin more effectively.

### The "Second Meal Effect"

The high soluble fiber content slows gastric emptying. This not only controls blood sugar after the current meal but can also improve glucose tolerance during your *next* meal.

### Metabolic Co-Factors

The blend is naturally rich in **Magnesium** and **Zinc** — trace minerals that many diabetics are deficient in, and which play a direct role in insulin synthesis and secretion.

### Weight and Satiety Management

This flour is nutrient-dense and high in fiber, promoting fullness much faster than refined flours. Managing caloric intake and body weight is a key factor in Type 2 diabetes reversal.

## Detailed Composition Analysis

- **Protein and Fat Profile:** The increased soy content raises protein to over 16g — approximately 50% higher than standard wheat flour. The fat content consists primarily of heart-healthy polyunsaturated and monounsaturated fats from soybean and millets.
- **Trace Minerals:** This blend is a powerhouse of Iron and Magnesium. Magnesium is a critical co-factor for over 300 enzymatic reactions, including those that manage blood glucose levels.
- **Fibre Content:** With over 10g of fibre, this mix exceeds the fibre profile of whole wheat. This high fibre density is the primary driver for a lower Glycaemic Load, as it physically slows the conversion of starch into sugar.
- **Bone Health:** The inclusion of Ragi ensures high calcium delivery — often a concern in gluten-free or restrictive diabetic diets.

## Estimated Glycemic Profile

- **Glycemic Index (GI):** ~48–49 — *Low GI* (anything below 55 is considered low)
- **Glycemic Load (GL) per 50g serving:** ~12.5 — *Medium GL* (range: 11–19)

By keeping the GI under 50, your body processes the sugar from these rotis at a very slow, manageable pace. The higher protein (16g) and fibre (10g) content act as a natural brake for glucose absorption.

## The Role of Fats in Further Reducing the GI

Adding a small amount of healthy fat — like **Ghee** or **cold-pressed oil** — to your dough has a direct scientific impact on blood sugar.

When mixed into the dough before cooking, fats coat the starch granules in the millets and chickpeas. This creates a physical barrier that:

- **Slows enzyme access** — digestive enzymes take longer to break down starch into glucose
- **Delays gastric emptying** — fat signals your stomach to release food into the small intestine more slowly, naturally lowering the GI of the entire meal

### Recommended Fats (for 1kg mix, add 1–2 tablespoons when kneading)

- **A2 Ghee** — Best for high-heat cooking. Contains butyric acid, great for gut health and reducing inflammation.
- **Extra Virgin Olive Oil** — High in polyphenols and monounsaturated fats, excellent for heart health — a major concern for diabetics.
- **Cold-Pressed Mustard Oil** — Traditional pungent flavor with a good ratio of Omega-3 to Omega-6 fatty acids.

Adding 1–2 tablespoons of fat to the blend can nudge the GI down to ~45–47, placing it in the "Extra-Low" GI category and making it one of the most diabetic-friendly staple flours possible. As a bonus, fat acts as a natural softener for gluten-free doughs, making rotis more pliable and preventing them from becoming hard as they cool.

## Preparation Note

Since this mix is entirely gluten-free, the dough will lack the elasticity of wheat. Knead with **warm water** and allow the dough to rest for at least **15–20 minutes** to improve binding and texture for softer rotis.
