# Fresh Recipes: Garden Salad with Satwik Greens

A simple and delicious salad recipe using fresh produce from our farm. Perfect for a healthy lunch or dinner side.

## Ingredients
- Fresh mixed greens from Satwik Farms
- Cherry tomatoes
- Cucumber
- Red onion
- Your favorite dressing

## Instructions

1. Wash all vegetables thoroughly
2. Tear the greens into bite-sized pieces
3. Slice the tomatoes, cucumber, and onion
4. Toss everything together
5. Add your favorite dressing just before serving

The key to a great salad is fresh ingredients, and our residue-free produce makes all the difference!
