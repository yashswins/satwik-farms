# Meet Our Cattle: The Heart of Our Dairy

Get to know the stars of Satwik Farms - our well-cared-for cattle who provide the freshest dairy products.

## Our Happy Herd

At Satwik Farms, we believe that the quality of our dairy products starts with the quality of life we provide for our cattle.

### How We Care for Our Cattle
- **Spacious Pastures** - Plenty of room to roam and graze
- **Quality Feed** - Nutritious, natural diet
- **Regular Health Checks** - Ensuring every animal is healthy and happy
- **Clean Environment** - Well-maintained facilities

Our cattle aren't just livestock - they're part of the Satwik Farms family, and we treat them with the respect and care they deserve.
