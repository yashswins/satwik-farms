# Farm Visit Highlights: School Trip Success

Last week we hosted students from Dar es Salaam International School. See photos and hear about their amazing farm experience.

## A Day at the Farm

The students had an incredible time learning about sustainable farming practices and getting hands-on experience with farm activities.

### What They Learned
- How we grow vegetables without chemicals
- The importance of soil health
- Where their food comes from
- Caring for farm animals

It was wonderful to see the excitement in their eyes as they explored the farm and learned about sustainable agriculture!
