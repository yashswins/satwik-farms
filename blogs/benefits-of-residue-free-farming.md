# The Benefits of Residue Free Farming

Learn why residue free farming matters for your health and the environment. We explain our chemical-free approach to agriculture.

## Why Residue Free?

Residue free farming is more than just a farming method - it's a commitment to health and sustainability. Here's why it matters:

### Health Benefits
- No chemical residues in your food
- Higher nutritional value
- Better taste and freshness

### Environmental Benefits
- Healthier soil
- Better water quality
- Supports biodiversity

At Satwik Farms, we're proud to practice residue free farming, ensuring that what you eat is as pure and natural as possible.
