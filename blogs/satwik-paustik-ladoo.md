Yes, in moderation. The sweetness comes only from natural whole food sources — no refined sugar — and the ingredients are primarily low glycemic. Diabetics are advised to have ½ to 1 small ladoo in the morning and monitor their response. Consult your doctor if unsure.

**Can children eat Paustik Ladoo?**
Absolutely. It is one of the best natural energy foods for growing children — rich in calcium for bones, healthy fats for brain development, and fibre for digestion. ½ to 1 ladoo a day is ideal.

**Is it gluten-free?**
Yes, our Paustik Ladoo contains no wheat or gluten-containing grains.

**How is it different from regular store-bought ladoos?**
Most commercial ladoos are made with refined sugar, maida, and artificial preservatives. Our ladoo uses only whole, natural ingredients — no refined sugar, no additives, and no shortcuts.

**What is the shelf life?**
As it is made with natural ingredients and no preservatives, we recommend consuming within the period indicated on the packaging and storing in a cool, dry place.

**Can it be eaten daily?**
Yes. One ladoo a day is a nourishing daily ritual — not an occasional treat. It is designed to be a consistent part of a healthy lifestyle.
