# Behind the Scenes: Our Dairy Process

Take a look at how we produce fresh milk, yoghurt, and ghee from our happy, healthy cattle.

## From Farm to Table

Our dairy process starts with the health and happiness of our cattle. We believe that happy, well-cared-for animals produce the best quality dairy products.

### Our Process
1. **Morning Milking** - Fresh milk collected daily
2. **Quality Testing** - Ensuring purity and freshness
3. **Processing** - Making yoghurt and ghee
4. **Delivery** - Fresh to your doorstep

Every step is done with care to ensure you get the freshest, highest quality dairy products possible.
