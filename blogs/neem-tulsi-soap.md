## The Powerhouse Ingredients

This soap brings together some of the most revered herbs in Indian Ayurvedic tradition:

![Fresh neem leaves — antibacterial, anti-acne, and deeply purifying](/images/neem-leaf.jpg)

### Real Extract of Fresh Neem Leaves
Known as *Azadirachta indica*, neem is a renowned blood purifier and anti-acne agent. It contains active compounds like **Nimbin** and **Azadirachtin**, which are highly effective against acne-causing bacteria and skin infections. Neem has been used in Indian households for centuries as a first-line remedy for everything from pimples to fungal rashes.

### Real Extract of Fresh Tulsi Leaves
*Ocimum sanctum* — Holy Basil — is a powerful antioxidant and anti-inflammatory herb. Tulsi acts as a natural detoxifier, clearing skin pores and promoting a radiant, even glow. In Ayurveda, it is celebrated as an adaptogen that helps the body and skin recover from stress-induced damage.

### Virgin Coconut Oil
Cold-pressed and unrefined, virgin coconut oil provides deep, lasting moisturisation — penetrating the skin barrier to prevent dryness, a common complaint with traditional neem soaps. Its natural antimicrobial properties also help maintain a healthy skin microbiome.

### Glycerin
A natural humectant that draws moisture from the environment into the skin, keeping it soft, supple, and hydrated long after every wash.
